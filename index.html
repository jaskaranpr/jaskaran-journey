Even as a kid I was into electronics and computers, and I cared about how things looked.

Electronics came first. I wanted to know what was going on inside the circuit.

Then computers, and the question changed to what I could make on one.

In April 2022 I joined Peepal Co in Bengaluru as a frontend developer, starting on CoinSwitch.

Then I moved to Lemonn, our stock market app for India.

SDE-1 in 2022, SDE-2 in 2024, SDE-3 now.

At Lemonn I wrote our candlestick chart from scratch in Flutter, with CustomPainter instead of a plugin.

Then the tools traders expect: trendlines, RSI, moving averages, Fibonacci, Heikin Ashi, zoom on both axes.

You can drag take profit and stop loss straight on the chart.

Then a backtesting engine on historical data, and strategies that send live signals over WebSocket. Watch the demo.

The indicator list kept growing: ATR, OBV, ADX, VWAP bands, OI profile and more, all behind one settings sheet.

I wrote a Kotlin native module that skips the React Native bridge to hold 60 FPS, and an order chain that handles over 1,000 updates a second.

I led our Android app's move from React Native to Flutter. In 2026 the chart went into the CoinSwitch app too.

In April 2026 I started Zing Trade, a Chrome extension.

It puts our strategies on top of TradingView charts on other platforms, beyond Lemonn and CoinSwitch.

You can trade them through Dhan, Groww, Kite, Upstox, Angel One, Fyers, CoinDCX and more.

I wrote most of it: the signals sidebar, live P&L, auto exit on stop loss or take profit, and encrypted storage for each broker.
