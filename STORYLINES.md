# Storylines

Three different films for the HyperFrames rewrite. Pick one (or a mix, which counts as one); the picked one becomes `SPEC.md`.

Facts used, all from Jaskaran: Chakar near Jagraon, Punjab; the village government school; electronics and computers as a kid; Photoshop (jaskaran.psd), then video editing; websites from YouTube in class 11, falling for CSS; CodePen experiments (a phone, a bus, a tree that draws itself); a YouTube ad, Masai School in 2021, Instagram and YouTube clones; Peepal Co in Bengaluru in 2022, CoinSwitch, then Lemonn (charts drawn from scratch, planning a trade on the chart, testing it on past days); Zing Trade (strategies inside Groww, Kite, Upstox and Dhan); Jassi (watches the app at night, wakes the right person); now SDE-3, back on CoinSwitch giving it a new look.

## 1. One line (recommended: it turns the one thing he cares about, how things look, into the camera itself)

**Device:** a character does the work, and the character is a single white line. **Form:** one continuous take; the camera never cuts.

A white line draws the horizon of Chakar's fields at dawn, and the camera never lets go of it: it folds into a school notebook, a Photoshop selection, a video timeline, a CSS border, a phone's edge, Bengaluru's metro track, Lemonn's price line, a cliff edge, a Zing Trade toggle, Jassi's heartbeat and CoinSwitch's new screen, then pulls back to the same horizon. You feel one hand and one eye for detail carried across ten years without a single cut.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 is a blue field with a white horizon line already half drawn at 45% height and a small white boy on a bicycle riding along it; by 2 s the line reaches the right edge, wheat stalks spring up off it, and "Jaskaran Singh" sets in at the left.
- **Beats:** Chakar horizon → notebook (government school) → lamp wire into a radio he opens (electronics) → Photoshop's marching ants, layers lift off (jaskaran.psd) → video timeline → YouTube progress bar (class 11) → one CSS line restyles the whole frame ("I fell for CSS") → CodePen tree draws itself → YouTube ad bar → Masai, the Instagram clone's feed → metro track into Bengaluru, Peepal Co, CoinSwitch → Lemonn's price line, candles, a trade planned on the chart → the chart's peak becomes a cliff: the leap → into white: Zing Trade's panel, broker chips → night: Jassi's heartbeat spikes and wakes the right person → dawn: the line draws CoinSwitch's new look → pull back to the Chakar horizon, "I still want things to look right" → end card.
- **Look:** Fold's two colours only: ground #0d3673 with a #1d498b radial key from upper left, ink #fdfdfd; the line is the one accent, 4 px white; Figtree 600 at 7% of frame height for titles, 400 at 3.2% for lines; a pen line on blue paper with 3% grain; the fuzzy white silhouette only at the start and the end.
- **Sound:** a slow felt-piano and pad bed around 70 bpm, quiet through childhood, opening at Bengaluru, dropping to near silence on the leap and resolving at dawn; soft key ticks on the CSS line, a pop on each broker chip.

## 2. Through the screen

**Device:** the result first, then where it came from. **Form:** an endless zoom; every world is inside a screen of the one before.

It opens on today, the new CoinSwitch on his laptop in Bengaluru, and dives back through screens, each one a year earlier: Jassi's alert on his phone at night, Zing Trade, the Lemonn chart, the Instagram clone at Masai, CodePen, a YouTube tutorial on a family computer, a Photoshop canvas, a cyber café monitor near Jagraon, until the last screen is a window onto Chakar's fields and a boy on a bicycle. You feel the years peel back until the film ends where he began.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 is the new CoinSwitch screen filling the frame, white UI on blue, the cursor resting on a button; by 2 s the camera is pushing into the button's gloss and his face appears reflected in it.
- **Beats:** CoinSwitch now (SDE-3) → dive into the phone: Jassi wakes the right person → into the alert: Zing Trade over Groww, Kite, Upstox, Dhan → into a candle: Lemonn's chart, the leap → into the office window: Bengaluru 2022, Peepal Co → into the phone: Masai 2021, Instagram and YouTube clones → into a pen: CodePen's tree → into a tab: class 11, CSS → into the canvas: jaskaran.psd, video editing → into the café monitor: the kid who loved electronics → out of the window: Chakar, the government school → end card.
- **Look:** each world is a real screen framed by its device (laptop, phone, monitor, CRT), toned to Fold's blue and white; Figtree 600 for the year stamp at 9% of frame height, one per world; every zoom passes through glass with a 1.5% reflection; grain 3%.
- **Sound:** a reversed-feeling ambient bed that gets simpler and warmer the further back it goes, ending on a single piano note over field sounds; a soft whoosh on every dive, two or three takes rotated.

## 3. The words become the things (the bold one)

**Device:** type carries the story. **Form:** one frame that rebuilds itself and never cuts away.

Giant Figtree words fill the frame and turn into what they name: "Chakar" grows wheat from its letters, "Photoshop" splits into five layers that fan out, "CSS" changes one property and the whole frame restyles, "Instagram" tiles into a feed, "Bengaluru" rises into towers, "Lemonn" becomes candlesticks, "leap" drops off the bottom of the frame, "Zing" snaps into toggles, "Jassi" stays lit as the frame goes to night, and "CoinSwitch" redesigns itself letter by letter. No figure at all; it's a typographer's film about someone who cares how things look.

- **First 2 s:** frame 0 is "Chakar" in white at 40% of frame height on Fold blue; by 2 s wheat ears are pushing up out of the tops of the letters and a thin horizon slides in under them.
- **Beats:** Chakar → "government school" set like a notebook heading → "electronics" wires itself → "Photoshop" layers → "video" gets a playhead → "CSS" restyles everything → "CodePen" → "Masai" / "Instagram" feed → "Bengaluru" towers / "Peepal Co" → "Lemonn" candles → "leap" falls → "Zing Trade" toggles and broker names → "Jassi" at night → "CoinSwitch", redesigned → "Still that kid from Chakar" → end card.
- **Look:** Fold blue #0d3673 and white #fdfdfd swapping ground and ink by chapter; Figtree 700 at 30–45% of frame height, -3% tracking, one word on screen at a time; the medium is type set in metal and then broken apart, no gradients, 2% grain.
- **Sound:** a pulse at about 96 bpm, every word change landing on a beat, the music cutting to silence for "leap" and coming back at half time for "Jassi".
